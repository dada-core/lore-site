## What an article holds

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Problem or question | What needs solving. |
| Context | The system involved, the environment, the circumstances. |
| Symptoms | What can be observed: error messages, behavior. |
| Causes | What triggers the problem. |
| Solution | The steps applied. |
| Precautions | What to check or avoid. |
| Related resources | The source documents: runbooks, incident reports… |

## Review cycle

An article goes from *Draft* to *Awaiting review*, then to *Approved* once a space owner approves it. Only approved articles are ranked first in search.

## Create an article

- From the knowledge base, with *New article*.
- From a document, for example an incident report.
- From an answer of the assistant <span class="vb">v0.3</span>: the article is filled in and must be reviewed.
