---

At the end of the [initial setup](initial-setup.md), Lore suggests these four steps. Your account owns the organization: it can access everything.

1. **Invite your team**
   Click *Invite people* in the sidebar, choose the role, then send the link yourself, by chat or by email. No mail server is needed. See [Members and invitations](members.md).
2. **Adjust security**
   In *Administration › Security and sign-in*: minimum password length, attempts before a lockout, lockout duration, session expiry and how long invitation links stay valid. See [Security and sign-in](security.md).
3. **Check the public address**
   In *Administration › General*, the public address is the one used in invitation and reset links. You also set the organization’s name, default language and time zone there. When the address is set by `LORE_PUBLIC_URL`, it is shown and cannot be changed.
4. **Set up your account**
   In *My account*: name, email address, interface language, theme and password. The list of open sessions lets you close the ones you do not recognize.
