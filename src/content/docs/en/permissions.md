title: "Roles and permissions"
description: "Access is decided per organization, per space and, if needed, per document. It applies everywhere: lists, reader, downloads, search and assistant."
---

## Roles in the organization

| Role | Rights |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Every right, including over the other owners and the organization’s settings. |
| Administrator | Access to every space, management of members and settings. |
| Member | Access to the spaces they belong to, according to their role in each. |

## Roles in a space

| Action | Reader | Contributor | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read, search, download | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Import, edit, create versions | No | Yes | Yes |
| Change a document’s status | No | Yes | Yes |
| Manage the space members | No | No | Yes |
| Archive and delete | No | No | Yes |
| See every restricted document of the space | No | No | Yes |

## Restricted documents

A document can be limited to a few people of the space. Other members do not see it anywhere: not in lists, search or excerpts. The space owners and the administrators always keep access.

> [!TIP]
> **Hiding a document from the space owners.** Put it in a dedicated space whose members are chosen one by one.

## Not found rather than forbidden

A document you cannot access returns the same answer as a document that does not exist: *This page cannot be found*. Even its existence stays confidential.
