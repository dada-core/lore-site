# Security Policy

This repository holds the website and the documentation of Lore, published on https://lore.dada-core.com: a static site, with no account and no form.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public issues, discussions or pull requests.**

- **In Lore itself**: follow the [security policy of Lore](https://github.com/dada-core/lore/security/policy) and use private vulnerability reporting on [dada-core/lore](https://github.com/dada-core/lore/security/advisories/new).
- **In the website** (for example a script or a link that could harm visitors): go to this repository's **Security** tab and click **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include a description of the issue and its potential impact, and the steps to reproduce it.

We will acknowledge your report, keep you informed of our progress, and credit you in the advisory if you wish.
